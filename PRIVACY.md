# Privacy Summary

CardWise AI compares estimated credit-card rewards using card terms and spending scenarios provided by a user.

The design avoids full card numbers, CVV, PIN, OTP, and issuer credentials. Planned controls include scenario deletion, export, account deletion, and configurable retention. Exact retention and backup behavior must be documented before launch.

The assistant should use source documents to explain terms. User spending data must not be sent to an AI provider without clear disclosure and an approved data policy. Training on user data is off by default.

This is a product-design summary, not a final legal privacy notice. Qualified review of applicable law, vendors, and actual data flows is required before launch.
