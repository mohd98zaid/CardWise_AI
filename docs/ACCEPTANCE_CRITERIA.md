# Acceptance Criteria

## Catalogue
Exact variants distinguishable; verification and last-verified date visible; active material rules linked to evidence; unverified rules not shown as verified; historical variants retained.

## Rules/calculator
Typed versioned effective-dated rules; publication requires evidence/tests/reviewer; Decimal arithmetic; cap boundary tests; explicit exclusion reasons; unknowns not silently zero; trace/manifest stored; identical inputs/versions reproduce output.

## Scenarios/optimizer
Scenario CRUD and duplication; comparisons and trace; planned spend preserved; ineligible allocations rejected; unknown cap usage visible; baseline/proposal assumptions recorded; optimizer method/version shown.

## AI
Material claims cited; unsupported questions abstain; prompt-injection tests pass; AI cannot publish rules; calculator works offline; model/prompt versions recorded.

## Security/privacy
Ownership and role checks; audit admin actions; no sensitive card credentials; secrets absent from logs/repo; exports expire; deletion/backup limitations documented.

## Operations
CI checks, health/monitoring, tested backup restore, migration/rollback docs, incident runbooks, source licensing review.
